Kid Achievements

An offline-first way for families to recognize good activities, manage points, and request meaningful rewards—with parents in control of every approval.

One app, two family roles

Parents and kids use the same app with separate profiles. A parent defines good activities and rewards, then can approve or reject each reported activity and reward request.

Motivation with clear points

A kid reports completed activities, sees pending and active points, and requests affordable rewards. Points become active only after parent approval.

Works offline by default

SQLite on the device is the source of truth. Core family features remain available without a network connection or a Vafrcor Studio synchronization server.

Optional encrypted synchronization

A family may connect its own Google account for Google Drive backup and multi-device synchronization. Cloud artifacts use the account's hidden app-data area and are encrypted with family key material protected by the synchronization passphrase. Vafrcor Studio performs no data mining.

Compatibility and support

Kid Achievements supports Android 7.0 / API 24 and newer. Distribution is private to known family members.
